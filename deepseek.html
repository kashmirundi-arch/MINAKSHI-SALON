About Us

Welcome to MINAKSHI SALON

Located in the heart of Sadar Bazaar, Chaibasa, MINAKSHI SALON is a ladies-only beauty parlour dedicated to providing exceptional beauty and hair care services. Our team of skilled professionals is committed to making every visit a luxurious and personalized experience.

From precision haircuts and advanced skincare to stunning bridal makeovers, we use high-quality products and modern techniques to bring out your natural beauty in a warm, comfortable, and hygienic environment.
